## Core Thesis

Delta Cosmology treats recursion and difference as co-conditions of observable
structure. The Babel Index is the engineering translation: a domain feature
family that measures whether coupling, convergence pressure, resource pressure,
agent density, and information velocity outrun differentiation and redundancy.

## Anti-Hype Rule

A successful NEOTH pilot validates the local LLM/agent telemetry instrument. It
does not prove the full cosmological reading. If the Babel score does not beat
raw telemetry and null models out of sample, the ratio structure is falsified
for that domain.
