Abstract

Delta Cosmology begins with the claim that neither difference nor recursion is sufficient as a primitive on its own. Difference becomes stable only through recursion. Recursion becomes distinguishable only through difference. Their co-condition is called RDelta.

The operational branch is the Babel Index: a domain-specific calibrated index family for loss of productive difference in bounded systems.

Babel Index

B_d(t) = norm_d((C_d * K_d * M_d * A_d * V_d) / (D_d * H_d + epsilon))

M_dResource or optimization pressure

H_dHeterarchy, redundancy, modularity

NEOTH Runtime Test

NEOTH is the first planned runtime testbed. The probe should observe orchestration events asynchronously, aggregate rolling windows, compute raw features, compute Babel variants, and compare them against strong baselines.

Falsification

If the Babel feature has no incremental out-of-sample signal after controlling for raw variables, the Babel-ratio structure is falsified for that domain.
